<h2 align="left">Hi, I'm Antonin Do Souto</h2>
<h3 align="left">Embedded Linux C/C++ Crash & Reliability Engineer</h3>

<p align="left">
I specialize in diagnosing and fixing <b>unstable, crashing, and hard-to-debug embedded systems</b>
written in C/C++, typically running on Embedded Linux in production environments.<br><br>

My work is focused on situations where systems:
<ul>
  <li>crash intermittently</li>
  <li>behave unpredictably under load or edge conditions</li>
  <li>contain years of accumulated technical debt</li>
  <li>are too critical or too expensive to rewrite</li>
</ul>

I intervene when teams are blocked, incidents are costly,
and traditional debugging approaches no longer work.
</p>

---

<h3 align="left">What I do</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Post-mortem analysis of crashes (core dumps, logs, runtime state)</li>
  <li>Root-cause analysis of instability and undefined behavior</li>
  <li>Race conditions, memory corruption, lifetime issues</li>
  <li>Stabilization of legacy C/C++ codebases</li>
  <li>Refactoring critical paths without full rewrites</li>
  <li>Definition and cleanup of system behavior and failure paths</li>
</ul>

---

<h3 align="left">Technical focus</h3>

<ul>
  <li>C / C++ (modern and legacy)</li>
  <li>Embedded Linux (Buildroot, Yocto-based systems)</li>
  <li>Crash dumps, instrumentation, diagnostics</li>
  <li>Deterministic logic and system behavior under failure</li>
  <li>Tooling, reproducibility, and hardening</li>
</ul>

---

<h3 align="left">What I deliberately do not do</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Web, SaaS, or application-level feature development</li>
  <li>UI work disconnected from system behavior</li>
  <li>Short-term “add a feature” freelance missions</li>
  <li>Greenfield projects with no production constraints</li>
</ul>

---

<h3 align="left">Working model</h3>

<p align="left">
I typically operate as part of a small senior engineering cell,
intervening for short, high-impact missions:
<ul>
  <li>diagnosis</li>
  <li>stabilization</li>
  <li>clear technical recommendations</li>
</ul>

I work primarily with international clients and accept on-site interventions
when they make economic sense, including in Asia.
</p>

---

<h3 align="left">Goal</h3>

<p align="left">
Build a sustainable, high-value consulting activity around embedded system reliability,
crash analysis, and long-term stability,
with a strong focus on financial independence and technical leverage.
</p>
